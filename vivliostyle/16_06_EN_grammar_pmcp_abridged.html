Pemecepe Creole Cheatsheets

A is B.

mi e elme
MI E ELME
I to be soldier
I am a soldier.

A does B. (intransitive verb)

alwe e lucuc
ALWE E LUCUC
festival to end
The festival ends.

For verbs that do not take a direct object, place an e before the verb.

A is at B. (existential)

mi mol jo dictic
MI MOL JO DICTIC
I to exist at/in/on house
I am at home.

Exception: the special verb mol (MOL) never takes an e before it.

jo (JO) is a panacea that can be used for ‘in’, ‘at’, ‘on’, etc.

A does B. (transitive verb)

mi kunlanit jeto
MI KUNLANIT JETO
I to eat/drink water
I drink water.

Verbs that can take an object end with it (IT).

Adjectives

xep naxe e xon
XEP NAXE E XON
big needle to be strange
The big needle is strange.

Adjectives take e (E) before them to become predicates. They can also be placed before a noun to modify it.

A noun modifying another noun

mi cet leti jutata
MI CET LETI JUTATA
we -’s voice
our voice

When a noun modifies another noun, leti (LETI) is basically required. Since this word appears very frequently, it is sometimes abbreviated as lt.

Pronouns

mi MI I
co CO you
ka KA this
ci CI that, he, she

Adding a suffix cet (CET) indicates plurality.

Preverbal auxiliary verbs

nip NIP do not
delu DELU need to do
cene CENE can do
celene CELENE want to do
mak MAK do again

If there is an e (E) before a verb, these are placed even further before it.

Postverbal auxiliary verbs

tude TUDE away from the subject
kije KIJE towards the subject

For example, by attaching a postverbal auxiliary verb to lijuit (LIJUIT) ‘to study’, you can express lijuit tude (LIJUIT TUDE) ‘to teach’ or lijuit kije (LIJUIT KIJE) ‘to learn’.

Conjunctions

pi PI and / but / and then connects sentences
no NO while doing connects sentences
at AT and connects nouns
o O or connects nouns
leti LETI -’s connects nouns
connects a sentence to a noun

In addition to its use when a noun modifies a noun, leti (LETI) can also be attached after a sentence to express complex ideas, in situations where English would use relative pronouns.

mi nipit leti zo
MI NIPIT LETI ZO
I to throw away -’s card, piece
The card (piece) that I discard

Note however that leti (LETI) is always omitted after the special verb mol (MOL).

jo ail mol auc
JO AIL MOL AUC
at / in / on Ai’r to exist all, everyone
all the people in Ai’r
everyone in Ai’r

Numerals

Numbers from 0 to 9 end with leti; the abbreviation lt is often employed.

0 0lt nipleti (NIPLETI) 5 5lt nokaleti (NOKALETI)
1 1lt panleti (PANLETI) 6 6lt xantaleti (XANTALETI)
2 2lt makleti (MAKLETI) 7 7lt xenleti (XENLETI)
3 3lt dukaleti (DUKALETI) 8 8lt lekkaleti (LEKKALETI)
4 4lt jupleti (JUPLETI) 9 9lt pentileti (PENTILETI)

Multiples of ten do not end with leti.
