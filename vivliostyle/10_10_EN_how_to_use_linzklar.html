INTRODUCTION

1. 見出し字

1-1. 収録字の選定

「日本机戦連盟 厳選燐字辞書」にて選定した 330 字程度（＋異体字 50 字ほど）のうち、4% に相当する 13 字（＋異体字 2 字）を抜粋して掲載した。

1-2. 分類と配列

二引形撃字機順で掲載されている「日本机戦連盟 厳選燐字辞書」からの抜粋であるため、同辞書に準拠した分類と配列を採用した。

1-3. 漢字転写

燐字文化に対する日本語話者の親近感を醸成する目的で、アイル共和国文化省対外広報処日本語部署はそれぞれの燐字の字体ごとに漢字をあてがい、「漢字転写」を定めている。（一例を挙げると、机戦という語を「机戦」と表記して「きせん」と読むといった行為は、この漢字転写に基づくものである。）日本語話者にとって字の意味や文の意味を概括する上で極めて有用であることを鑑み、この辞書では漢字転写を墨付きカッコの中に入れて表示することとした。

1-4. パイグ音とその表記

燐字はパイグ語のみが用いる字ではなく、パイグ語も燐字のみを用いる言語ではないが、品詞に対する解釈を入れずに燐字列を音声に起こす方法としてパイグ音での読み上げが広く行われている現状も踏まえ、パイグ語での慣習的な読みが安定している字についてはその読みを掲載した。

2. Parts of speech

それぞれの見出し語には、パイグ語での用例をもとに、品詞欄を設けた。その語形変化の乏しさも伴って、パイグ語の品詞論は未だ定説を見ないが、便宜上次のような基準を設けて品詞分類を行った。

2-1. What ‘falls short’ of being a word

We have recognised three types of elements that cannot appear independently within a sentence as standalone components: abbreviations, prefixes, and suffixes.

2-2. A word

Words denoting persons, things, and so forth, along with words exhibiting similar syntactic behaviour, are classified as nouns. Words denoting actions, states, and so forth, which form the core of a sentence, are classified as verbs. Among these, those exhibiting a strong tendency to express states and which, syntactically, particularly resists taking direct objects or the progressive marker 在 are specifically classified as stative verbs. Furthermore, verbs that take clauses as their direct objects are classified as clause-taking verbs. Words uttered to express emotion directly, rather than appearing within other sentences, are classified as interjections. Those with a strong tendency to modify the immediately following noun syntactically and semantically are classified as prenominals for convenience. Words serving to connect sentences are classified as conjunctions for sentences.

2-3. Divisible compound words

Numerous instances exist where multiple characters combine to form a single meaning, yet the constituent characters do not necessarily appear consecutively within the text, allowing space for other words to intervene. Whilst it would have been possible to adopt a policy of not recognising these as words and omitting them from the dictionary, grasping these kinds of ‘divisible compound words’ is indispensable for a thorough linguistic understanding of how sentences written in Lanermic logograms behave. We therefore endeavoured to include them proactively. Where each separable part can be clearly identified as a constituent element within a sentence, it has been denoted as such, e.g., [verb + object].

3. 語義と訳語

3-1. 語義の分類

語義には、先頭に品詞を記した。品詞が同一であっても、語義が大きく異なる場合については行を分けた。

3-2. Supplementary explanations

Supplementary explanations regarding the semantics of words are indicated in parentheses (). Where additional information concerning the frequency of the word itself in Pekzep or syntactic constraints is necessary, this is indicated in curly brackets { }. Furthermore, additional explanations have been provided on new lines where required.

4. Example sentences

Example sentences have been provided for some headwords to illustrate the usage of words concisely.
