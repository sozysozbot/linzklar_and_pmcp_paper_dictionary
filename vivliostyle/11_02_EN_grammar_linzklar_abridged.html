Pekzep Cheatsheets

A is B.

我 兵
pai2 kauk2
I soldier
I am a soldier.

You can also use ka1 to indicate the boundary between the subject and the complement, as in ‘pai2 ka1 kauk2’ (I am a soldier).

A does B.

我 口 水
pai2 iam1 nua2
I eat/drink water
I drink water.

The basic word order is analogous to that of English.

A is in B.

片 在 於 皇処
zuo1 aim2 ie tam2 hue
piece exists in tam2 hue (orange square on the game board)
The piece is in tam2 hue.

於 (ie) is a panacea that can be used for ‘in’, ‘at’, ‘on’, etc.

Tones

1 long; pitched high 直 io1 (straight)
豊 ho1 (abundant, wealthy)
2 long; pitched low
(tends to rise at the end)		 牛 io2 (cow)
骨 ho2 (bone)
(none) short; mid-level pitch 王 io (leader, king)
軟 ho (soft)

Mispronouncing tones seldom hampers communication.

Pronouns

我 pai2 I
汝 mua2 you
此 ka1 this
彼 źap2 that, he, she
其 źe1 that (contextual reference)

Adding the 等 (ge) suffix indicates plurality.

Auxiliary verbs

無 mun1 does not
在 aim2 is doing
与 tui2 makes someone do

Auxiliary verbs are placed before the verb.

Interrogatives

何 nan2 what
何人 nan2 cuk2 who
何時 nan2 kak when
何処 nan2 hue where
何故 nan2 xit2 why
如何 em nan2 how

Conjunctions

而 ua and then connects sentences
加而 at ua in addition, furthermore connects sentences
別而 pau1 ua however, but connects sentences
即 hem therefore, then connects sentences
加 at A and B connects nouns
亦 y or connects nouns
之 a -’s connects nouns;
connects a sentence
to a noun

The noun that follows an 之 (a) can be omitted.

Sentence-final particles

乎 yn2 is it?
噫 a ah
終 ta have finished

終 (ta) is also often placed immediately after a verb.

Numerals

  • −1: 下一 (ut2 et2)
  • 0: 無 (mun1)
  • 1: 一 (et2)
  • 2: 二 (ik2)
  • 3: 三 (om2, vulgarly om1)
  • 4: 四 (ap1)
  • 5: 五 (un1)
  • 6: 六 (net2)
  • 7: 七 (nik2)
  • 8: 八 (nom2, vulgarly nom1)
  • 9: 九 (nap1)
  • 10: 十 (nun1)
  • 100: 百 (kit1)
  • 1000: 十百 (nun1 kit1)
  • 10000: 万 (ue1)
  • 12: 十二
  • 26: 二十六
  • 525: 五百二五
  • 1212: 十二百十二
  • 2345: 二三百四五
  • 230045: 二十三万四十五
  • 876543: 八十七万六五百四三

Note the omission of 十 (nun1) triggered by the presence of 百 (kit1) nearby.
